Layout A: Foto først

  • Idea: stacked rows like the Press home page. Status strip where Press has its announcement bar, then a full-width photo, headline, buttons + practical info, brands, newsletter, footer.
  • Mobile order: 1 status · 2 photo · 3 headline + intro · 4 three buttons (≥44 px) · 5 hours (today highlighted) · 6 contact · 7 address · 8 brands · 9 newsletter + logo · 10 footer.
  • Password page code: ① status strip, ② buttons + hours + contact + address. Photo, headline, brands, newsletter and footer are theme sections.
  • Brands: 7 logos + tile = exactly 2 rows of 4. From 12 logos the grid switches to 6 per row by itself (try A 25), otherwise 25 logos become 7 rows.
  • Weak spot: the desktop photo is full width, so the 515 px temp photo looks soft until the real one arrives. On desktop the hours table sits below the first screen (the status strip answers "open now?" at the top).

Layout B: Foto og info side om side

  • Idea: the Press "Image with text" split (8/12 photo + 4/12 info). On desktop, photo, status, buttons and hours are all in the first screen.
  • Mobile order: 1 logo bar · 2 headline + intro · 3 photo · 4 status · 5 three buttons · 6 hours · 7 contact + address · 8 brands · 9 newsletter · 10 footer.
  • Password page code: ① one big block holding the photo and all info, because Image with text can't hold a table, three buttons or a live status. The photo then lives in code, not in the theme's image picker.
  • The logo bar is main-password at the top, so the newsletter needs the separate Newsletter section (unverified on the password page). 6 per row makes the wide Specialized wordmark small.

Both

  • Greys stand in for Press red #FF2E00 and pink lines #FFBFBF. The photo is greyscale only to keep the wireframe colour-neutral (colour vs. B/W is A7's call).
  • Fonts: fallback fonts (Times New Roman / Menlo). Real Instrument Serif is much narrower, so headings will take fewer lines.
  • Logos: height = base × "scale" from brands.json (36 px desktop, 26 px mobile), capped at 72% of the cell width, never stretched. MET is an almost square solid badge: at scale 1.4 it is ~50 px tall on desktop, never hits the width cap and sits centred with air around it. That is intended; do not enlarge it to fill the cell.
  • Static example: today = fredag 9 Oct 2026, status "Åbent nu · lukker 16:00". The live version needs JS.
